About Sawadee

Sawadee is a smart Thailand travel planner designed to save you time and help you book smarter. We aggregate the best options from 8 trusted partners so you can compare and choose what works for you.

Our Mission

Thailand is one of the world's most visited destinations, but planning a trip often means jumping between dozens of websites, comparing prices, reading reviews, and coordinating flights, hotels, and activities across multiple platforms.

Sawadee simplifies this. We bring everything into one place so you can compare transport options, accommodation across budget levels, and activities—all tailored to your dates and group size.

Transparency

We believe in being honest about how we work:

  • Prices are reference guides. We show historical averages to help you budget, but final prices are confirmed on partner sites.
  • We don't charge you. Sawadee is free to use. We earn small commissions from partners.
  • We redirect you to partners. All bookings happen on the partner's website—we never handle payments.
  • No hidden fees. What you see is what you pay (plus any taxes or fees added by partners).
