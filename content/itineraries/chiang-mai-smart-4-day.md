## Smart Money-Saving Tips (Chiang Mai Edition)

1. **Temples are mostly free** – only Wat Chedi Luang and Wat Phra Singh charge (B40-50).
2. **Use Grab Bike** for short trips (B20-40) instead of taxis (B100+).
3. **Book Sunday Walking Street early** – best food sells out by 8 PM.
4. **Doi Inthanon gets cold** – rent a jacket at the park entrance for B20 if you forgot yours.

## Traveler FAQs

**Is Chiang Mai cheaper than Phuket?**
Yes – meals are B40-80 vs B100-200 in Phuket. Accommodation is 30-40% cheaper.

**Do I need to book elephant sanctuaries in advance?**
Yes – ethical ones fill up 1-2 weeks ahead, especially November-February.

**Can I do Doi Inthanon on my own without a tour?**
Yes – rent a scooter (B200/day) but roads are steep. Tour is safer for most.

**What's the burning season?**
February-March – air quality is hazardous. Avoid these months.
