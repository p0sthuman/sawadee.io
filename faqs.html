Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about using Sawadee and planning your Thailand trip.

Is Sawadee free to use?

Yes, Sawadee is completely free. We don't charge you for using our planner or for redirecting you to our partners. We earn small referral commissions from partners when you book, which helps us keep the service free.

How accurate are the prices shown?

Prices we display are historical averages for budgeting purposes only. They help you estimate costs, but actual prices vary by season, demand, booking advance, and other factors. Always confirm final prices on the partner's website before booking.

Do I have to book through the partner links?

No. Sawadee is a planning tool—we simply recommend options and redirect you. You're free to search independently on any booking platform. We just make it faster by aggregating options in one place.

Does Sawadee handle payments?

No. Sawadee never handles payments. All bookings and payments occur directly on the partner's website (Agoda, Booking.com, 12Go, etc.). We simply redirect you and don't process any transactions.

What cities in Thailand does Sawadee cover?

We cover 15+ major Thai cities including Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Phuket, Koh Samui, Krabi, Chiang Rai, Ayutthaya, Hua Hin, Sukhothai, Koh Phangan, Koh Tao, Ko Lanta, Pai, Udon Thani, and Pattaya. We also support international departure cities like Singapore, Bangkok, Hong Kong, Tokyo, and more.

Can I plan a multi-city trip?

Currently, Sawadee helps you plan one main route at a time. Our Travel Packages tab shows suggested itineraries, waypoints, and multi-leg trips. For complex multi-city routes, you can run multiple searches and combine results.

What payment methods do the partners accept?

Each partner has their own payment options. Agoda and Booking.com typically accept credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal. 12Go accepts cards and bank transfers. Please check the specific partner's site for all accepted payment methods.

Can I get travel insurance through Sawadee?

Sawadee doesn't offer travel insurance directly. However, many partner sites (like Agoda and Booking.com) offer optional insurance at checkout. We recommend purchasing travel insurance independently before your trip.

Is my data safe?

Sawadee doesn't store your personal or payment information. We only process search parameters locally in your browser. When you click through to a partner, their privacy policy and security practices apply. See our Privacy Policy for details.
